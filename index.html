How It Works

From Profile to Plate in Seconds

The agentic pipeline turns your health data into actionable nutrition intelligence.

1

Build Your Profile

Enter your age, weight, height, activity level, goal, dietary preference, and any health conditions in the sidebar.

2

Choose an Agent Task

Select a tab — generate a meal plan, track macros, ask a nutrition question, analyse a recipe, or request health insights.

3

Prompt Construction

The app assembles a rich, context-aware prompt that embeds your entire profile alongside the specific nutrition task.

4

IBM Granite LLM Inference

The prompt is sent to IBM Granite-13B Instruct via the watsonx.ai REST API. The model generates a detailed, personalised response.

5

Actionable Output

Results are displayed in the app. Meal plans are also saved to plan.md for offline reference.
