## General Healthy Eating Principles

1. **Eat whole foods** — minimally processed grains, lean proteins, fresh produce.
2. **Hydrate well** — aim for 8–10 glasses (2–2.5 L) of water per day.
3. **Control portions** — use the plate method: ½ vegetables, ¼ protein, ¼ wholegrains.
4. **Limit added sugars** — keep below 10% of total daily calorie intake (WHO guideline).
5. **Reduce sodium** — target < 2,300 mg/day; less for hypertensive individuals.
6. **Eat mindfully** — eat slowly, recognise hunger and fullness cues.
7. **Regular meal timing** — helps regulate metabolism and energy levels.
8. **Limit ultra-processed foods** — these are linked to chronic disease risk.
